From fabric to finishing touches

Behind every doll

Every detail starts with a little imagination.

From the fabric I choose to the smallest finishing touch, I build each doll around the story you want to tell.

  • Fabric & finishesI use soft cotton or linen for the doll body, then choose outfit fabrics, lace, and trims that bring your idea to life.
  • Soft fillingPolyester fiberfill gives each doll a soft, cuddly shape and helps the little details sit just right.
  • Thread & face detailsI match thread colors to the design and use embroidery floss, fabric paint, and markers for expressive little details.
  • Needles & pinsEvery seam, outfit, and tiny detail is carefully positioned and hand assembled for a polished finish.
  • Finishing touchesButtons, bows, shoes, hats, and other accessories are selected to make each doll’s personality feel complete.
  • Your inspirationSend me a photo, color palette, or simple idea. I can work from a pattern or help turn your inspiration into a one-of-one doll.
